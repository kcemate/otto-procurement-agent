Every startup has a silent margin leak: SaaS seats, duplicate tools, legacy contracts, and renewals nobody owns.

This is Otto Procurement Agent. It audits a company's SaaS stack, finds wasted spend, and turns the savings into a business.

Hermes MoA runs the council. GPT-5.5 reviews the spend structure. GLM 5.2 Cloud challenges risk and implementation effort. GPT-5.5 aggregates the final paid audit.

Here is the loop. The agent ingests the SaaS stack, detects duplicate systems and low-utilization seats, ranks savings by effort and risk, and renders a buyer-ready audit.

Then it operates. It creates a success-fee checkout for twenty percent of first-year savings. It records revenue, MoA cost, ingestion cost, provisioning cost, hosting cost, and reinvestment in the next scan.

It also prepares the work queue: cancellations, migrations, seat true-ups, and routing plans. This is not a chatbot. It earns, spends, and runs procurement operations.
